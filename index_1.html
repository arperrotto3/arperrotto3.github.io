GTA6 Top 5 Daily

The five best GTA 6 moments of the day, in one short video.

What this app does

GTA6 Top 5 Daily is a personal content tool operated by Anthony Perrotto. Each day it finds the most-watched GTA 6 clips, edits them into a single "Top 5" compilation with original commentary and graphics, and publishes that video to the GTA6 Top 5 Daily accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

The app uses YouTube API Services, the TikTok API and Instagram's publishing tools to upload the daily video to the operator's own accounts and to read basic information about the videos it has uploaded. It does not access or post to anyone else's account.
