Privacy Policy

GTA6 Top 5 Daily · Last updated November 26, 2026

This policy explains how GTA6 Top 5 Daily ("the App"), operated by Anthony Perrotto, accesses, uses, stores and shares information. The App is a personal tool that creates a daily GTA 6 highlight compilation and publishes it to the operator's own social media accounts.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the App accesses

  • YouTube account access (operator only): When the operator signs in with Google, the App receives permission to upload videos to the operator's YouTube channel, set video titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read basic information about the channel and its videos (such as video IDs, titles and view counts).
  • Public video information: The App may read publicly available information about videos, such as titles, view counts and links, to select clips for the daily compilation.

The App does not collect personal information from viewers, followers or other YouTube users, and this website does not use cookies or tracking.

How information is used

Information is used only to create, upload and manage the daily GTA6 Top 5 Daily videos. It is not used for advertising, sold, or used to build profiles of any person.

How information is stored

Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer and are not sent to any other server. Any YouTube API data the App stores is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.

Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share Google or YouTube user data with third parties. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the App's access to a Google account at any time through the Google security settings page. When access is revoked, the stored sign-in tokens stop working and are deleted from the App. To request deletion of any other stored data, email the contact below; requests are completed within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
